## Workflow

Classify the check before choosing a technique. Validate recovered rules with positive and negative vectors. A public verification key cannot sign arbitrary payloads. Changing a client key or verifier does not make the original signature valid or change a backend's decision. Keep captured private keys, tokens and personal identifiers out of reports and commits.

Use supplied or explicitly scoped targets. Example commands do not authorize unrelated actions. Record tool versions, artifact hashes, observations, limitations and verification actually performed. A successful parser, schema check or byte edit does not verify an entire procedure.
